When your user experiences a connectivity issue visiting your website, it’s unlikely that you’ll see a trace of it in your server logs. DNS resolution failures happen before your user ever connects to your server. TCP timeouts, on shared infrastructure such as Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), are difficult to attribute to a specific origin.
